Farming

This page shows how yield farming works on the Ethereum Sepolia testnet. Here you can stake your LP tokens (from AST/APT or AST/WETH pools) to earn APT rewards over time. It demonstrates the core DeFi mechanic: providing liquidity, staking LP, and harvesting earned tokens.

Deposit LP Tokens - stake your LP/AA or LP/AW tokens to start earning APT rewards.

Farm info:
  • RPS (Reward per Second) — shows how many APT tokens are distributed every second across all farmers. The higher this value, the faster rewards accumulate.
  • Pending - your unclaimed APT rewards. This number grows in real time while your LP tokens are staked. You can harvest it anytime.
  • Staked LP - the amount of LP tokens you’ve deposited into the farm. The more LP you stake, the bigger your share of the total rewards.

Functionality:
  • Withdraw - remove your LP tokens anytime; your rewards stay safe.
  • Harvest - claim your accumulated APT without unstaking.

What it gives:
You earn APT passively for supporting liquidity in the ecosystem. The longer you keep LP tokens staked — the more rewards you collect.
