Liquidity

This section shows how liquidity pools work in DEX. Here you can add tokens to a pool to earn fees, remove them anytime, and see your share in real time. By providing liquidity, you become part of the AMM, enabling swaps between tokens like AST/APT and AST/WETH.

Each pair represents a liquidity pool of two tokens that users can swap between — like AST/APT or AST/WETH.
When you add liquidity to a pair, you receive LP tokens that represent your share in that pool.

Tokens from Pairs
  • Pair AST/APT gives you LP/AA tokens.
  • Pair AST/WETH gives you LP/AW tokens.
Select Pair — choose which pool you want to interact with (AST/APT or AST/WETH).
Pair Info:
  • Reserves - shows the current pool size
  • Total Supply - total LP tokens
  • My LP - your share

Add Liquidity
  1. Token 1 / Token 2 - enter how much of each token you want to deposit.
  2. The form keeps the correct ratio automatically.
  3. After adding liquidity, you’ll receive LP tokens (like LP/AA or LP/AW) representing your share in the pool.

Remove Liquidity
  • LP Token Input - enter how many LP tokens you want to withdraw.
  • You’ll get back both tokens (AST + APT or AST + WETH) according to your pool share.

In Result:
  • Adding liquidity = you provide tokens to help the swap system work and earn fees.
  • Removing liquidity = you get your tokens back plus the small fee portion you’ve earned.
