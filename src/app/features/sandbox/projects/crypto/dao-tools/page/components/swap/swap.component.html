Swap

This demo shows how decentralized token exchange works on the Ethereum Sepolia testnet. You can swap between AST, APT, and WETH tokens using an automated market maker (AMM) (just like in Uniswap) and explore how prices, liquidity, and fees are calculated in real time.

AMM (Automated Market Maker) is a smart contract that allows users to trade tokens directly without the need for an order book or centralized intermediary.

It relies on liquidity pools — reserves of two tokens provided by users (ex. AST and APT). When you make a swap, the AMM automatically adjusts the token prices based on the pool ratio, ensuring constant liquidity for every trade.
The swap form lets you choose two tokens, set the amount you want to exchange, and instantly see the calculated rate, fee, and minimum amount you’ll receive.

You can also flip tokens, adjust slippage tolerance, and preview real-time swap info before confirming the transaction.

Form data:
  • Slippage – protects you from price changes during transaction.
  • Amount input – token amount you sell.
  • Select From / To – choose tokens you want to swap between.
Swap info:
  • Min received – lowest amount you’ll get after slippage.
  • Price impact – how much your swap moves the pool price.
  • Fee (0.3%) – trading fee taken from your input amount, not a gas fee.
