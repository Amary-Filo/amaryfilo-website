AMFI DAO Tools

This is a demo project that showcases the core mechanics of decentralized finance within the AMFI ecosystem on the Ethereum Sepolia testnet. Here you can provide liquidity to token pairs, earn rewards through farming, and experiment with token swaps, experiencing how automated market makers (AMMs) and yield protocols work in practice.

I built and designed everything myself: the smart contracts, the site, and the UI, using the latest tools and frameworks. All tokens, rewards, contracts, and auctions are only on the testnet, so you don’t spend real money.

AST is a Amfi Special Token.
AST is the base utility token of this demo. You can get it from the faucet or by swapping. Use AST to provide liquidity (e.g., AST/APT, AST/WETH), trade on the AMM, and generally move value through the system.
APT is a Amfi Pure Token.
APT is harder to obtain directly. The main way to get it is by staking LP/AA in the Farm to earn APT over time. You can also swap for APT if liquidity allows. In the broader ecosystem, APT unlocks premium features and items.
LP/AA is a AST/APT Liquidity Token.
When you provide liquidity to the AST/APT pool, you receive LP/AA tokens. They represent your share of the pool and entitle you to 0.3% of trading fees plus APT farming rewards. You can stake them in the Farm to earn extra APT over time.
LP/AW is a AST/WETH Liquidity Token.
Providing liquidity to the AST/WETH pair gives you LP/AW tokens. These represent your share in the AST/WETH pool and accumulate 0.3% swap fees. Although they are not used in the APT farm, they can be redeemed anytime for the underlying AST and WETH.
WETH is a Wrapped ETH Token.
WETH wraps native ETH into a standard ERC-20, making it compatible with the AMM and LP math. You can swap between WETH and other tokens, or provide liquidity (AST/WETH). The router also enforces safety knobs around WETH (like reserve floor and daily caps) to protect pools.
The Router orchestrates interactions with pairs: it routes trades, transfers tokens into the pair, mints/burns LP on liquidity changes, and enforces safety rules (price-impact limit, WETH reserve floor, daily WETH cap). Approvals are granted to the Router so it can move your tokens for these actions.
The Factory deploys new Pair contracts and records them. When you request AST/APT, it returns the canonical pair address. The Router relies on the Factory to find pools, ensuring swaps and liquidity operations always hit the right contract.
The Farm distributes APT rewards to users who stake LP/AA tokens. The longer you keep them staked, the more APT you earn.
You can:
  • Deposit LP/AA to start farming.
  • Harvest to claim pending APT.
  • Withdraw LP/AA anytime.

It’s a passive income layer built on top of liquidity providing.
Each pair (e.g., AST/APT, AST/WETH) holds reserves of two tokens. It follows the constant-product AMM model. Liquidity providers deposit both tokens at the current ratio and receive LP tokens representing their share. Traders swap against the reserves; fees (0.3%) accrue to LPs via the pool state.
What to do:
  • Network: Ethereum Sepolia testnet.
  • Wallet: Connect MetaMask. Make sure you have a little SEP ETH for gas.
  • Get AST: Use the faucet (recommended) or Swap (WETH/AST).
What to do:
  1. Goal: Convert between AST, APT, WETH using the AMM.
  2. Pick pair: “From” = the token you sell; “To” = the token you buy.
  3. Enter amount and review Min received / Price impact / 0.3% fee → Swap.

Notes:
  • You may see Approve prompts the first time you trade a token.
  • Respect the pool’s price-impact guard and the WETH safety rules (the router can reject risky trades).
What to do:
  • Goal: Provide tokens to a pool and receive LP tokens.
  • Choose pair: AST/APT or AST/WETH.
  • Enter “Amount Token 1”: the form auto-quotes Token 2 to keep the pool ratio.
  • Confirm approvals (once per token) then Add Liquidity and you receive LP/AA (for AST/APT) or LP/AW (for AST/WETH).
What to do:
  • Goal: Stake LP/AA to earn APT over time.
  • Deposit: Enter LP amount and click Deposit.

Watch:
  • RPS = APT / second emitted to all stakers
  • Pending = your unclaimed APT
  • Staked LP = your LP/AA in farm.
  • Harvest: Click Harvest anytime to claim APT.
  • Withdraw: Get your LP/AA back (rewards are automatically settled).
What to do:
  • Goal: Redeem LP back into the two underlying tokens.
  • Enter LP amount (or use 25/50/75/100%) then Remove Liquidity and receive Token 1 + Token 2.
  • Open Auction then you’ll see Active, Withdraw, and Past pools.
Swap:
  • From / To: Pick sell/buy tokens.
  • Amount: How much you sell.
  • Slippage: Max price drift you accept.
  • Swap Info:
  • Min received — worst-case output after slippage,
  • Price impact — how much your trade moves the pool price,
  • Fee (0.3%) — taken from input; not the gas fee.

Liquidity:
  • Select Pair: Choose AST/APT or AST/WETH.
  • Reserves / Total Supply / My LP: Pool state and your share.
  • Add Liquidity: Enter Token 1; Token 2 auto-quotes; confirm.
  • Remove Liquidity: Enter LP to redeem the two tokens back.

Farming:
  • RPS: APT emitted per second to all stakers.
  • Pending: Your unclaimed APT.
  • Staked LP: Your deposited LP/AA in the farm.
  • Actions: Deposit LP/AA → Harvest APT → Withdraw LP/AA.
“Approve” keeps popping up: the first time for each token & spender is normal. After that, you won’t see it until the allowance is insufficient.
  • Pending transaction: open it in the block explorer; if gas was too low, speed it up from your wallet.
  • Wrong network: switch to Sepolia and refresh.
  • Decimals / min amount errors: the input enforces token decimals and minimums; adjust the value until the error disappears.
