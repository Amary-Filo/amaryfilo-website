About project

This is a test project that shows how DeFi works on the Ethereum Sepolia testnet. Here you can try the full cycle of a decentralized app: get AST tokens from the faucet or staking, join auctions to win APT tokens, and spend them in the marketplace on demo items.

I built and designed everything myself: the smart contracts, the site, and the UI, using the latest tools and frameworks. All tokens, rewards, contracts, and auctions are only on the testnet, so you don’t spend real money.

AST is a Amfi Special Token. The base token of the ecosystem. This token is the starting point of the whole project flow.

You can get it:
  • for free via the faucet (ast contract).
  • or multiply it through staking (lock tokens to earn interest).

The main purpose of AST:
  • participate in staking to grow your balance,
  • use it in auctions: place bids to win APT tokens.
APT is a Amfi Pure Token. It is the rare and exclusive token of the this project ecosystem.

You cannot get it from faucet or staking. The only way is to win it in auctions, by bidding with AST.

The main purpose of APT:
  • it is the utility token used in the Marketplace.
  • only with APT you can buy guides, components, private channels, or other digital goods.
Staking is a way to multiply your AST balance over time.

Features:
  • You choose how long to lock your AST tokens (from 15 minutes to 3 hour in this demo).
  • The longer you lock — the higher the percentage reward.

Example calculation:
  • Assume the APR is 4% per year.
  • If you stake 100 AST for 1 year: you would earn 4 AST.
  • If you stake the same 100 AST for 15 minutes (≈ 1/35,000 of a year), your reward will be: 100 * 4% * (15 minutes / 1 year) ≈ 0.00011 AST.
  • At the end, you withdraw: 100.00011 AST (your initial + reward).
APT (Amfi Pure Token) cannot be minted or farmed. The only way to get APT is to win it in auctions.

How it works:
  • Each auction is a timed pool where users compete with bids in AST.
  • When the timer ends — the highest bidder wins and receives the APT reward.
  • All other bidders get their AST refunded automatically.

Rules:
  • You can only bid with AST tokens.
  • Minimum bid = 1 AST.
  • Each new bid must be higher than the previous one.
  • If you lose, your AST is refunded instantly.
  • If you win, you must wait until the pool ends to claim your APT.
The Marketplace is where you spend APT.

How it works:
  • Each item has its price in APT.
  • Some items are digital downloads (PDFs, code, components).
  • Other items are links (exclusive access, private channels, tools).
  • After buying an item, it moves to your “My Items” section.

Rules:
  • Only APT tokens are accepted.
  • Items can have different types:
    • Download: instantly get the file.
    • Link: access to a private resource or channel.
  • Some items can be purchased multiple times.
  • Purchases are on-chain events: once confirmed, the item is yours forever.

Example:
  • You have 2 APT from auctions.
  • You buy an item priced at 1.5 APT: it goes into “My Items”.
  • Remaining balance: 0.5 APT.
  • Next time you open the Marketplace, your purchased item is available under “Your items”.
What to do:
  • Switch your wallet to Ethereum Sepolia network.
  • Gas: make sure you have a little test ETH for gas fees.
  • (Optional) Add tokens to wallet: add the contract addresses for AST and APT so you can see balances.
What to do:
  1. Click Connect Wallet on the top bar.
  2. Approve connection in MetaMask (or your wallet).
  3. Once connected, the app will load your balances and on-chain history.

Tips:
  • If you see “Wrong network”, switch to Sepolia in your wallet and reload the page.
  • Balances and lists auto-refresh when your status changes to connected.
AST (Amfi Special Token) is your working token:
  • Get it from the Faucet (if enabled), and/or
  • Earn more via Staking (next step).
What to do:
  1. Open Staking.
  2. Enter the amount of AST to stake and choose a duration.
  3. Click Stake: your wallet may ask for a one-time Approve (allowance), then the Stake transaction.
  4. After the timer reaches 00:00, click Withdraw to receive your initial AST + reward.

Notes
  • Longer durations have higher APR, so you earn more AST.
  • Positions become withdrawable only after the lock ends.
  • The UI shows your positions and timers; when it hits 00:00 the card switches to Withdraw.
What to do:
  1. Open Auction → you’ll see Active, Withdraw, and Past pools.
  2. Pick an Active pool. Check:
    • End in: the countdown.
    • Top bid: current highest AST bid.
    • Min to outbid: the minimum you must bid to become the new leader.
  3. Enter your AST amount and click BID.
    • First time: your wallet will prompt Approve AST: Auction.
    • Then confirm the Bid transaction.
  4. If you’re outbid, your previous bid becomes refundable automatically by the contract; the UI will reflect your new status.
  5. When the timer ends:
    • If you are the highest bidder, the pool moves to Withdraw: click Withdraw to receive APT.
    • Otherwise, you’re done (you’ll see the pool under Past).
What to do:
  1. Open Marketplace → browse Available items (each shows a price in APT).
  2. Click Buy on an item:
    • First time: your wallet will prompt Approve APT → Marketplace.
    • Then confirm the Buy transaction.
  3. After the purchase confirms, the item moves to Your items:
    • Download items give you a file (e.g., .zip, .pdf).
    • Link items grant access to gated resources.

Partial payments (supported)
  • If you pay less than the listed price (on-chain), the app will count it as a partial payment and show Remaining on the card (e.g., “Paid 0.5 of 2 APT → Remaining 1.5 APT”).
  • Click Buy again later — the app will automatically pay only the remaining amount.
Auction: you’ll see live updates when bids are placed, pools end, and when your status changes to Withdraw.

Marketplace: the app listens to Purchased events and updates Your items and Remaining automatically.

Balances: AST/APT balances update after each confirmed transaction
“Approve” keeps popping up: the first time for each token & spender is normal. After that, you won’t see it until the allowance is insufficient.
  • Pending transaction: open it in the block explorer; if gas was too low, speed it up from your wallet.
  • Wrong network: switch to Sepolia and refresh.
  • Decimals / min amount errors: the input enforces token decimals and minimums; adjust the value until the error disappears.
