Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and handled when you use this website and submit the contact form.

1. What data may be collected

When you submit the contact form, the following information may be collected:

  • Your name
  • Your email address or contact details you provide
  • Your message

Technical metadata may also be included with the submission for basic context and abuse prevention, such as:

  • Current page URL
  • Page title
  • Referrer URL
  • Browser language
  • Timezone and timestamp
  • UTM parameters, if present
  • User agent

2. Why this data is collected

Submitted information is used only for purposes directly related to communication, such as:

  • Responding to your message
  • Reviewing a role, collaboration request, or technical inquiry
  • Following up on relevant frontend or product-related opportunities
  • Basic spam prevention and context review

3. How the data is processed

Form submissions are processed through third-party infrastructure used to operate the website and deliver email notifications. This may include hosting, serverless functions, and email delivery providers.

Submitted data is not sold, rented, or used for advertising purposes.

4. Third-party services

This website may rely on third-party providers to support hosting, delivery, and contact form processing. Depending on the current implementation, this may include services such as:

  • Vercel — hosting and serverless infrastructure
  • Resend — email delivery for contact form submissions
  • Cloudflare — DNS, security, and traffic protection

These providers may process technical request data as part of normal website operation.

5. Data retention

Contact messages are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for communication, review, and record-keeping, unless a longer retention period is required for legitimate administrative or legal reasons.

6. Your rights

You may request access to or deletion of personal information you have submitted through the contact form. To do so, use the contact details provided on this website and include enough information to identify your message.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the website, contact workflow, or legal requirements. The latest version will always be published on this page.
